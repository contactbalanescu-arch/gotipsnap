Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the standard tipping rate in the United States?

The standard tipping rate for sit-down restaurants in the US is typically between 15% and 20% of the pre-tax bill. For exceptional service, 22% or more is common, while 10% to 12% is usually reserved for below-average service.

How do you split a bill evenly with a tip?

To split a bill evenly, take the total amount (including tax and tip) and divide it by the number of people in your party. TipSnap automates this process so everyone knows exactly how much to pay via cash, card, or apps like Venmo.

Do you calculate tips before or after tax?

Etiquette dictates that you should calculate the tip amount based on the pre-tax total of your bill, not the final amount after local sales tax has been added.
