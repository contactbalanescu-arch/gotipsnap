Why Use Our Free Tip Calculator?

Using TipSnap is simple. Just enter your total restaurant or bar bill amount, choose the percentage you wish to leave, and select the number of people sharing the expense. Our online split bill calculator will instantly find the exact gratuity amount and the grand total per person.

How do you split a bill evenly with a tip?

To split a bill evenly, take the total amount (including tax and tip) and divide it by the number of people in your party. This clean tip calc automates this process so everyone knows exactly how much to pay.
